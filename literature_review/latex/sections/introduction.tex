\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

This survey examines regional English accent as an evaluation dimension for
speech-to-text translation. Its focus is a comparison between two operational
system categories. A \emph{Direct speech translation} (Direct ST) system maps
source-language speech to target-language text without exposing an
intermediate transcript. A \emph{Cascaded speech translation} (Cascaded ST)
system first applies automatic speech recognition (ASR) and then machine
translation (MT). These definitions establish the terminology used throughout
the survey; they do not, by themselves, imply that either category is more
accurate or robust.

The survey is organized around the following research question:
\begin{quote}
\emph{How does regional English accent affect the translation quality of
Direct and Cascaded speech-to-text translation systems?}
\end{quote}
The intended comparison concerns final translation quality across regional
accent groups. ASR word error rate (WER) is treated only as a diagnostic for
the Cascaded ST pathway, not as a substitute for measuring translation
quality.
