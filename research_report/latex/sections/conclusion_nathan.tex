\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We compared a Direct system (SeamlessM4T v2) with two Whisper-to-NLLB
cascades on 4,200 English recordings from seven regional accent groups,
scored against Chinese references. On this frozen set, Direct
outperformed both cascades on all four quality metrics and within every
accent group. The larger NLLB checkpoint narrowed the gap. Accent groups
differ in score within each system, but by less than Direct differs from
either cascade. Higher Whisper WER was weakly associated with lower
cascade scores. What remains inconclusive is whether any system is more
robust to accent. The design cannot separate accent from speaker and
sentence content, or architecture from model size and training data.

In practice, these results favour Direct on this benchmark for all seven
groups, but they do not justify choosing a system for a particular accent
without testing on that accent's speakers and judging translations with
human raters. Accent group mattered less than system choice, yet group
differences and the Direct lead both varied, so small groups need more
speakers before they can be compared reliably.

We propose the following studies. These are future plans, not completed
or validated improvements. Each names the limitation it addresses.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Broader replication} across more models, language directions
and datasets. This addresses the single direction and checkpoint set.

\item \emph{Matched speaker and content design}, with the same sentences
read in each accent, verified accent labels, and speaker and sentence
dependence modelled jointly. This addresses unequal content, speakers
and recordings, repeated sentences, and self-reported labels.

\item \emph{Human translation-quality evaluation}, with several
references or post-editing. This addresses single-reference automatic
metrics.

\item \emph{Controlled tests that change one ASR or MT component at a
time}, extending our 600M and 3.3B comparison. This addresses the
model, training and capacity confounds.

\item \emph{Held-out evaluation of any marker mitigation or fallback
rule}, on data not used to design it. This addresses the post-hoc,
subset-based diagnostics.

\item \emph{Fuller replay records}, with pinned commits, saved
environments and shareable derived data. This addresses the incomplete
reproducibility records.
\end{enumerate}
