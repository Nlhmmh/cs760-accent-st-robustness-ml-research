\section{Experiments and Results}
\label{sec:results}

All three full inference runs completed successfully for 4,200 clips, with no
recorded inference failures or generation-limit flags. The results establish
the overall and per-accent quality ranking, assess uncertainty and accent
variation, and examine associations with recognition errors. Marker diagnostics
then investigate unusual Direct outputs, before sensitivity checks test whether
the ranking persists under alternative subsets and speaker weighting. These
additional analyses remain separate from the frozen benchmark comparison.
Finally, saved runtime records summarize the computational cost of full inference.
